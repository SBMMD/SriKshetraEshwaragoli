Welcome

Welcome to the official website of Sri Brahma Mugera Sri Mahankali Daivastana, Eshwaragoli - Hosabettu. This site is dedicated to our sacred traditions and cultural heritage.

This sacred Daivastana serves as a spiritual center for the worship of several revered Daivas of the Tulu Nadu tradition, including Ullaya, Mahankali, Brahma Mugera, Thangadi, Alera Panjurli, Swami Koragajja, and Daivaraja Mantra Guliga.
